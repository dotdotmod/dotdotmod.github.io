By the by (lol), it's still not rigged yet (because I haven't done her jacket or her shoes yet), but I decided to do something different with the eyes so I used an alpha card over her eye whites to get the effect. It's pretty smart, however, the downside is that it makes it impossible to use outside of blender, and I'm still not sure if I want to give out this model, I might actually back track on this decision.. These kinds of tricks are usually saved for animation/movie specific models, not optimized game-ready models (which I've been making this model with that in mind).

There's something I really need to get off my chest that's been boiling and developing inside me for the last 1 and a half months. For the last month and a half, I haven't made any substantial piece of art. Or anything really. And when I say substantial, I don't even mean a full game, or a large scale drawing, or a full song. Even just a small thing. Well.. I did make a 3D model of Shadow, but it was basically a reskinning of Sonic's model which means it didn't take any large amount of work.

Here's a picture ::

It's technically not done yet (the underside of his shoes aren't finished), and like I said, it didn't take that much effort to make.

But anyways, I don't think you can call what I'm facing "art-block". To me, art block is when I as an artist refuses to accept my own ability to express and portray my abstract thoughts. In fact, I'm probably the best at art than I've ever been. But I still haven't drawn in a week. The last time I drew was a quick warm-up to see if it really is art-block; I drew a few small real-life referenced human sketches and I was fairly content with how they looked (They were still 100% flawed, but I felt fine with it) and I moved on with my day. So now you might be thinking, "maybe you don't have any ideas?". But the think is, I DO have ideas. Lots actually... ^^;

I don't like talking about my ideas and big thoughts publicly, but while washing my clothes the other day, I had this idea of this maid-like character who wasn't a maid because she had to, but was one because she enjoyed being one. She really liked washing clothes by hand and would hang them on these huge, super long lines attached to tall trees, and she was able to balance on them like a ninja.. She was a textbook of knowledge when it came to germs, what temperature of water to use, what kinds of tools to use, and the material of different kinds of cloth.

Sounds cool doesn't it?? I wanted to the clothes lines attached to the trees..... but I haven't!!!!!

So now I have the suspicion that it's actually just lazyness. That I just don't want to put in any kind of work into working on ideas. Even if they're this small. Even if they wouldn't take much effort. This entry was also supposed to come out last month, but I've failed to make one in time, so now here I am now. Crying about it to you ;_;
