Also, speaking about looks, I wanna kinda talk abt the site's looks. It's very simple and somewhat retro (retro as in old website-style). It uses Courier New as the main font (some of you may not notice depending on the platform, i.e. PSVita) which is usually associated with old typewriters and such. It's also just a classic in terms of Fonts, up there with Arial, Comic Sans, Calibri, and Papyrus as one of my favourite classic Windows fonts. I like Impact too, but I don't think it's every been bundled with Windows before, because I had to download it.. Truely unfortunate.. On the other hand though, this website is almost dissapointingly simple. There's really NOTHING special here. Nothing. No fancy CSS, No complex Structure, Static backend... Lame aesthetic....... Cringey human being behind every word........... I hope you find something to like about this site despite all the shortcomings.
