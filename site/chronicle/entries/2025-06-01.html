Ah! I know! Let me talk about Megami Tensei!

My first exposure to Megaten, I believe was when I first got my PS Vita, I decided to check out the PlayStation store and download some game trailers so I could have something to do aside from play games. Two of the trailers I got were for Persona 4 Golden, the first one was the game's intro cutscene and the other was a gameplay trailer. When I watched the intro, I honestly didn't know how to feel. Back then, I had practically 0 exposure to JRPGs, let alone anime style games in general. It looked interesting, mysterious, but I also knew it wasn't for me. After all, I think I was only about 8 or 9 when I watched it. I liked the song and thought the visuals were interesting, but the hung up dead bodies and Igor's ominous prescence drew me away from the game. The second trailer only pushed me further away from the game. It showcased the game's gameplay which didn't interst me all too much. It showed some social links, beach scenes, anime girls, weird animal mascot, TV worlds, anime girls, anime boys, anime girls.. Things that all made me cringe (that I have now come around to liking lol). A few days later, I deleted the trailers in an attempt to forget about what I had seen, which worked actually. But about a year or two later, I was visiting my cousin's place and while we were there, I played some games on my older cousin's PSP, while he took a look at the stuff on my Vita. A nice exchange if you ask me. While I was looking through his digital library, I found a game called "Persona 3 Portable". The name "Persona" rung a bell in my head, but it wasn't enough to fully remember. I started a new save file, got though the opening cutscene, and immediately dropped the game after Pharos appeared. I think the Yukari """"""suicide"""""" scene got me worried at what kind of game I could possibly getting myself into, so seeing a weird little kid weiring prison attire at this so called "dorm" asking me to sign a contract while there's a bunch of coffins lying all around just outside the dorm was what set me off. Yeah, just a hunch lol. My cousin tried to reassure me that the kid becomes your friend later on in the game's story, but I was too shaken to really care. I quit the game and tried to never remember what just happened.

At this point, I didn't exactly have the most positive experience with this "Persona" series.. So, I never really played any game, or payed any attention to it for the longest time.. And so, the seasons changed..

A few years later, I'm watching one of my favourite YouTubers, QuinBoBin playing The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild, and when the video ends, I see an end card for his series on a game called "Persona 5 Royal". For whatever reason, this didn't ring any bells in my mind. I just thought, "Huh. Never played THAT before. Lemme see what this game is about...". I click on the video, and I'm greeted by.. a game. A pretty interesting looking game. Actually, a REALLY interesting game.. It's actually pretty cool looking... I continue watching the series, episode after episode. Getting hooked on the game's art style, music and story. Of course, I wasn't playing the game though. I was watching a playthrough. But I was still really hooked on what I was seeing and hearing, so I look into it. And from then on, my life was never the same. I went back and played Persona 3 Portable, and it somehow turned into my #1 favourite game of all time, with all the other Persona games trailing not too far off from that spot. I had just never really played a game like it before. (Well.. I HAD technically played it before, but.. yeah.) The characters, the music, the visuals. Even on that crusty old Huawei phone I was emualting the game on, I still loved the game thoroughly. At the time, I was in my early teen years and I was struggling really badly with life in general so the game's themes and story really struck a chord with me. I don't think I'll ever find another game that resonates with me as well as P3P does, but this wasn't the end of my Megaten journey!

Being in the Persona fandom for almost a year at that point, it was inevitable that I would find out just how deep the series' roots go. Although my memories on it are a whole lot more foggy, I remember finding out about the wider Megami Tensei series relatively naturally so I don't have a cool discovery story.. But I do have a really interesting story that only recently concluded!

..that I can't tell just yet! Because this is where this entry ends sadly...
