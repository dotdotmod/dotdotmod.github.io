This year, I've seen a huge influx of people sharing a common vibe about Minecraft and a common phenomenon dubbed "The 2 week phase". Basically, you come back to Minecraft after a long time away, enjoy playing the game a bunch for 2 weeks, slowly run out of steam, then stop playing for a few months before repeating the cycle. It's something that many people have come to hate a bunch, seemingly because they want to have the same kind of infinite joy and comitment that they had when they were younger of constantly playing in the same world for months and months, never getting bored or losing excitment. There have been many ways in which people have tried to combat this, one of which is a concept called "forever worlds", where... you guessed it, they play on this world forever. Never starting a fresh, constantly building on, and on, until they literally can't anymore. It's very simple in concept, but I wonder how many people will actually be willing to do this. I most modern Minecraft updates, the game is structured in a way where the more progress you make, the less challenge there is. In a typical gameplay loop, the player gets stronger and stronger by interacting with the games mechanics and in response, the game also gets harder and more challenging to keep things fun, until the game ends. Minecraft is basically the same. You start with nothing, get wood, go underground, get stone, get iron, get diamonds, go to the nether, find a fortress, get blaze rods, kill endermen, make eyes of ender, go to the stronghold, go to the end, and kill the ender dragon. The End..... or is it? Of course, Minecraft is a game that prides itself on infinite worlds and it's practically endless nature, so what's next? Well, I'd like to discuss my theory on how Minecraft is structured and how it creates a disconnect for many people.

Minecraft is split into two halves that create one full. The Creative side and the Adventure side. On one side, the game is treated like an empty canvas for the game to be painted on, and on the other side is a well built Adventure game, with a steady and challenging progression system that offers lots of replayability. Together, these two halves create the brilliant masterpiece that is Minecraft. Neither side can be done on it's own, otherwise an incomplete experience will be had. Therefore, to play Minecraft to it's fullest is to express yourself and your ideas in your world, while advancing through the game's mechanics and becoming a stronger, more powerful player. THIS is the full essence of Minecraft. Keep in mind, I didn't explicitly say "Creative Mode" or "Survival Mode", because I feel like this theory can apply to both actually (Though that's a whole other can of worms I haven't though about enough to really express in written for just yet.. ).

In many videos that talk about Minecraft becoming boring, they reminise on their childhood days of when they could play Minecraft all day without getting bored for a second, always wanting to play, always learning new ways to enjoy the game. They then go on to talk about how something has changed in the game itself that no longer makes them feel this way.
