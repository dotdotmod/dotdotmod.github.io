Man.. This SUCKS!!!!

Just this morning, I was feeling really chill and confident and happy with myself and the current state of my life, now I'm back to wallowing in sadness. My flaws more abundent than ever. My frustrations and pains taking over once more. And it's only made worse by the boiling hot weather here. Summer is the worst weather to live through. I like all seasons from a sort of 3rd-person spectator kind of way, but in my kind of living situation, it just totally sucks.

Bugs and frogs come out of hiding, a billion mosquitoes all attacking at once, my laptop overheating like hell.. so on and so forth. I think I might start taking showers twice a day now, the heat is just that much of an inconvinience :(.

It doesn't help that I'm slowly reaching the end of the line in terms of my current living situation too. Just 2 more years and I'll be out of time. I've burnt through another year of my life fumbling around, knowing what I want to be, but failing to make more progress towards that goal. I want to be a game developer. I want to make a living creating art. Doing the thing that I enjoy. At the beginning of this year, I set 4 goals for myself. Make 4 cel-shaded anime models, finish one game of any size, reach JLPT N3 by X-mas, and improve on studying habits for not just school, but also drawing, japanese and anything I set my mind to.

I'd like to say I've completely failed. That I'm a total hack, and a fraud who doesn't deserve to be alive. That I should just pack it up, write my suicide note and leave. ..but the reason side of me knows it's not over. That I'm not a total failure. I haven't made 4 anime models, but I still have 3 months till the end of the year. That's around 90 days. If I manage to do one simple design per month, I can probably do 1 model every 2 weeks and still make it out with plenty of time. My Japanese is far from a lost cause too, it's the best it's ever been. Although I've been far too lazy to do Anki for the past 3 months, I've been immersing myself by watching plenty of Japanese livestreams and content online to the point where I've noticed my retention level of words has improved by a whole bunch. I haven't done any JLPT mock tests in a while, but I think I might be able to do an N4 one with alright results now. I still need to get to that.

As for the release one game thing, I almost did. 3 days before Cirno day (September 9th) I was working on a Touhou fangame with Cirno in it. It was basically bowling mixed with a 3D platformer (so not bowling at all ^^;) and I was drafting out the dialouge and art when I realized how little passion I actually felt for the whole game I was making. It didn't feel genuine. I wasn't making it because I thought it was a particularly interesting idea for a game or anything, I was just trying to pump out something for Cirno day so I could get that little bit of validation from the rather low standards of people in the Touhou game-dev community. (Not that I've critisizing this! I'm too lzy to go into nuance with this rn lolololol) They would likely completely forget about my game after a month. I don't want to make that kind of game. I want to make something that can be loved and cherished, something that makes someone think differently than before, something people can think about, and talk about for years and years to come. We live in an age where there's never been more 'content' to consume than before. So many games to play, so many movies to watch, so much art to look at, so much music to listen to, so much stuff. Too much for me to keep my own expectations so low.

I don't care much about making the best game either, what I care about is making an interesting game. And I know I can't do that with a tight deadline and a shallow plan. My experience working on that game has taught me that.

However, the process of making this kind of game is lonely and depressing. Everyone is taking part in game jams, so many challenge videos on YouTube like 'I made a game in XX days~!!' and what-not. I also don't want to share my ideas before I've compeleted the majority of the game either, so it's not like I can get any shallow praise from people saying things like 'This is what AAA games should look like!!!' over an unfinished tech-demo I make. Many times, I get frustrated with myself. I have an idea in my head, that I try to make a reality, but I fail. And with that pain of failure, I realize all the other failure I've made along the way, and suddenly my winning streak of happiness breaks apart. I collapse to my knees looking at my spagehtti code, my ugly 3d models, my aimless game-design, my lack of sound and music... and I throw away the project. It's so hollow inside me too. It's not like I make some kind grand declaration like I'm ending a long term relationship. I just feel so sad. I feel like I wasted my time. I feel like a fake game developer. I feel like I'm worthless. I feel nothing. And I try. I try to fix the problems. I try to keep pushing. But where can a hollow husk of a human get the energy to fight on? I'm a social wreck.

I spend most of my time in my room, typing away on my laptop, doodling and scribbling in my sketchbook, the only person there being myself. The only social interaction I get is the shallow, energy draining interactions I have with the people I call friends at school, and the addictive, dangerous, 1-dimensional experience that is social media.

...............

Gosh, I'm getting a bit tired here.. I think I'd rather do an audio log thing. Sorry for failing to type this out :(. I'm feeling like a wreck emotionally, so it's difficult articulating all of these thoughts into full sentences and stuff. I think it's best I speak rather than type here. :::
