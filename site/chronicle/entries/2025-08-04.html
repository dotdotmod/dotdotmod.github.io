A few weeks ago, I started replaying Pokemon Emerald with my little sister. I'm pretty worried that since she has her own phone, she'll be consumed by the modern, attention span lowering social media, so playing a slightly longer game with her that she actually really likes will hopefully counter this in some way. I also plan to play Black and White or Platinum after this, so that might be pretty fun too! It's actually been super fun so far! There's just something really special sharing the nostalgia I have with that game to someone who actually cares that's just so heartwarming and lovely. She really likes Pokemon, just in general too. I used to let her play LeafGreen on my modded PS Vita a while ago and she really just fell in love with it all. According to her, the reason why she likes it so much is because of how little it demands of the player in terms of reflexes and general difficulty. There's just loads to enjoy as a casual gamer or a kid who still isn't very good at video games yet.

It's also just been really nice to return to the Hoenn region as a long time fan of Generation 3. The poppy visuals, salty sea smell.. the lovely loud trumpets~.. Hoenn is truely home to me. I grew up emulating GBA games on my Laptop and eventually PS Vita so games like Pokemon Emerald, Wario Ware, The Legend of Zelda: Minish Cap and Metroid Zero Mission are all super nostalgic to me. But Pokemon Emerald especially, was my #1 favourite out of all of those. I remember playing it almost every single day, getting through Gyms, exploring the region, trying to complete my PokeDex, performing a bunch of glitches I saw on YouTube... those were such fun times.....

I'm already working on an entire game, one which I really want to get out by November.

So yeah, that's my current state when it comes to gaming.

I'm really in the mood for making some 3d models ngl..... I sometimes just get the itch to do it, but never know what to make. I was going through a bunch of games I have, thinking to myself : "Which game has a cool character I can make". But as I rejected all of my games one by one, I realized the real reason I want to make a model. Clout. I know, it's a bit shameful, especially from Mr. Art-guy over here.. but the feeling of validation and praise from other people is just so strong. it's crazy. About an hour ago, I uploaded the Kat head model to a discord server I'm in, and people liked it! And that made me feel really happy! And I wanted to make more..! I think this is why, despite having friends in real life, I still feel so empty on the inside. It's because I know they won't like what I do. It's because I'm afraid they'll think I'm weird. It's because they WILL think I'm weird. I often play up this cool-guy persona IRL sometimes so I can justify not talking to other people, but then it places the expectation that what I DO like has to be something extraordinary, amazing or incomprehensible to them. Even in reality, what I enjoy is Sonic the Hedgehog, cute japanese anime girls, and vtubers. I want to open my heart so badly, to feel that acceptance from someone, anyone. But I know I can't. Because if I do, nobody will like me. If I do, all the progress I've made to start talking to real people again will be destroyed.

To bring this back to the game I'm working on, I think this is why I'm feeling burnt out. My own expectations for myself are just too high, and because nobody is there to keep me in check, I've just been spiriling into this kind of soft-depression where I try and force myself to work on it, but nothing good comes out and in response, I force myself to work harder. Which results in burnout. It's hard making something you like, when the only thing you truely want is the validation of someone who doesn't exist.

Sorry for getting really heavy all of a sudden, but yeah.... that's how I feel...................
