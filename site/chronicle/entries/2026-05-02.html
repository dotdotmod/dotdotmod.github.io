Next topicc.... I've recently gone full throttle back to learning Japanese! I originally started in late 2022, then unfortunately stopped in early/mid 2024 because the book I had been writing in to study got stolen. Then throughout 2025 (last year) I would attempt to return again.. but the feeling of loss from losing that book was pretty bad, which hit my motivation alot. You might think that this was a silly reason to lose motivation, but the book itself was actually a gift from a special group of people (can't tell ya lol) who I'll likely never see again... but I know I can't let that get in the way of my own personal dreams and passions, so here I am again. Pushing onwards. It's been a while, so I'm really rusty when it comes to simpler Kanji and grammar that I've already learned, but I've been also been catching up pretty quickly.

In terms of vocab, I'm probably around N4 level, but when it comes to grammar I'm still stuck in N5 hell.... ;-;. Though, I'd say that Japanese grammar isn't actually all too different compared to English.. so as long as I understand the vocab, I'm usually able to make some pretty accurate assumptions based on the context and what not. Everyday since around the 16th of April, I've been studying 60+ flashcards of Kanji using Anki so I can help boost my vocab level. There's this deck called Core 2000 which, just as the name implies, cover the most common 2000 Japanese words and their Kanji. Although as of late I've been considering switching to a smaller deck that has fewer, but more important and common Kanji. Especially ones with Kanji that have super common radicals, which can help me recognize and understand even the complicated one without really needing to have studied them seperately.

When I first started on the 16th April, I did about 53 reviews, which I thought was alot... until I was researching what's a good amount of time to spend, and found that some people out there are doing 300 per day!! Good grief!!!! "I'm never going get to native level ;_;" ..I thought to myself... but then on the night I was supposed to study, I pushed myself to the limit and managed to do 163 reviews in a little under 50 minutes, setting a new best time and review record for myself. I think when it comes to doing something mentally taxing every single day, over and over, it's best to try and "game-ify" the whole process by keeping track of your progress or adding elements of randomness (which Anki does both kinda automatically).

But mann... I totally over did it that day.... on the three days following that my review count was on a steady decline.. From 163.. to 100... to 83... to only 62.... I'm so tired... But I seriously need to keep trying to get higher and higher. I can't settle for only 30 minutes of study time daily, I need to raise it! I need to push myself harder and harder until I can clear at least 150 reviews daily without breaking a sweat. That's my goal...

As fo immersion, I've switch the language on all my devices to Japanese so I can be forced to read and comprehend it on a daily basis. I've also been trying to play more Japanese games in their native language.. but to be honest, I haven't really been playing much in general lately so.. yeah ^^; I do also want to try forcing myself to consume more content online in Japanese but that's been really hard to do because of the nature of online content in general... It's intended to be more "pickup and consume" if that makes sense (Just grab your phone and start consuming) and also due to how algorithms are optimized to recommend whatever keeps you on the site longer, which is hard to break because I'm already super comfortable with English making it super easy to get addicted to watching videos in the language for hours and hours, versus Japanese which due to my lack of experience, can leave me drained after even just 20 minutes of trying to understand what I'm hearing/looking at. Although, that is kind of the whole point of that kind of immersion. It's meant to be taxing and push your ability to comprehend the language. The problem is I'm just so lazy ^^;

So yup. That's it :)
